# Bird song credits

The birds on the family tree sing when tapped. Each song is a few seconds of a recording from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/)
under a free licence, trimmed to its first phrase, evened in loudness and re-encoded as a small mono MP3. The app lists them under
Guide → Picture and sound credits. Our copies of the CC BY-SA recordings are shared under the same licence as the original.
